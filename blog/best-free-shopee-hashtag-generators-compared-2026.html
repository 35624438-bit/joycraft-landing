Why Trust This Comparison?

I have been selling on Shopee since 2019 across three stores—fashion accessories, phone cases, and home goods. I have tried manual hashtag research, spreadsheets, and every tool on this list. The rankings reflect actual usage, not affiliate deals.

Every tool was tested with the same product: a "stainless steel water bottle 750ml" listed in the Home & Living category on Shopee Malaysia. I ran each tool and recorded the hashtag suggestions, how relevant they were, and how long the process took.

1. PostBoost AI — Best Overall (Chrome Extension)

PostBoost AI is a Chrome extension that sits right on your browser. You open a product page on Shopee, Lazada, or TikTok Shop, click the extension icon, and it generates a title, description, and hashtags—all in one go.

What I liked:
  • Speed. From clicking the button to getting 15 hashtags: about 4 seconds. No copying and pasting product details. It reads the page automatically.
  • Platform-specific suggestions. Hashtags for Shopee skew toward search-friendly terms like #WaterBottle and #StainlessSteelBottle. For TikTok Shop, it adds trending tags like #TikTokMadeMeBuyIt. That matters because what works on Shopee does not always work on TikTok.
  • Multi-language support. English, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese, and Chinese. If you sell cross-border in Southeast Asia, this alone makes it worth trying.
  • Title + description included. Most hashtag tools stop at tags. PostBoost generates the full listing copy—title, bullet points, description, and hashtags—in one shot.
What I did not like:
  • Free tier caps at 3 uses per day. If you list 10 products daily, you need the paid plan ($3.90/month).
  • Browser-only. No mobile app. If you manage your store from your phone, this is less convenient.
  • The AI sometimes overuses emojis in descriptions. You can edit them out, but it is an extra step.
Pricing: Free (3x/day), $3.90/month, $29/year, or $49 lifetime. Best for: Sellers who list across multiple Southeast Asian platforms and need everything (title, description, hashtags) generated in one click. Try PostBoost AI for free

2. SellerPic — Best for Analytics Nerds

SellerPic is a web-based tool that does more than hashtags. It has a full keyword research dashboard with search volume estimates, competition scores, and trend graphs.

What I liked:
  • The analytics dashboard gives you actual numbers. You can see which hashtags have search volume and which are dead.
  • It saves your search history, so you can build a hashtag bank over time.
  • Supports English and Indonesian.
What I did not like:
  • Slower than browser extensions. You have to copy your product title, paste it, and wait 8-10 seconds.
  • The free tier shows ads, and they are intrusive.
  • Hashtag suggestions sometimes pull in unrelated terms. For my water bottle, it suggested #Coffee—technically related, but not what a buyer would search.
Pricing: Free (5 searches/day), paid plans from $9.90/month. Best for: Sellers who want data-backed hashtag choices and do not mind a slightly slower workflow. Try SellerPic

3. BigSeller — Best If You Already Use BigSeller

BigSeller is primarily an order and inventory management platform. Their hashtag generator is a side feature inside the listing tool. If you already run your Shopee store through BigSeller, it is convenient. If not, it is overkill.

What I liked:
  • Integrated with the listing workflow. You create a product and get hashtag suggestions without switching tools.
  • Supports English, Indonesian, and Thai.
  • 10 free uses per day, which is generous.
What I did not like:
  • You need a BigSeller account and you need to use their listing interface. It is not a standalone hashtag tool.
  • Suggestions tend to be generic. For the water bottle, it gave me #Bottle, #Water, #Drink, #Home. Technically correct, but not competitive.
  • The interface is cluttered. Too many menus.
Pricing: Free (10 uses/day within the listing tool). BigSeller paid plans start at $29/month. Best for: Existing BigSeller users who want hashtags without adding another tool. Try BigSeller

4. Hashtagify.shop — Best for Unlimited Free Use

Hashtagify.shop is a simple web tool. You type a keyword, it returns 20-30 related hashtags. No signup needed. No daily limit on the basic generator.

What I liked:
  • Truly unlimited free use. No account, no cap.
  • Suggestions are ranked by popularity (low, medium, high).
  • Clean, no-distraction interface.
What I did not like:
  • English only. If you sell in Thailand or Vietnam, this tool is useless.
  • No platform awareness. It treats Shopee hashtags the same as Instagram hashtags. The suggestions for my water bottle included #WaterBottleChallenge and #DrinkWater—stuff that works on Instagram but nobody types into Shopee search.
  • No product context. It only looks at the keyword you type, not the actual product listing.
Pricing: Free. Premium plans with analytics start at $12/month. Best for: English-only sellers who need unlimited hashtag ideas and do not mind filtering out irrelevant suggestions. Try Hashtagify.shop

5. ShopeeTags.io — Simplest Interface

ShopeeTags.io does exactly one thing: you type a keyword, pick a category, and it returns hashtags. No extras. No analytics. No description generation.

What I liked:
  • Dead simple. The whole process takes under 10 seconds.
  • Category-aware. Selecting "Home & Living" gave me slightly more relevant tags than the untargeted tools.
  • Works for TikTok Shop too.
What I did not like:
  • English only.
  • Only 3 free uses per day.
  • Suggestions are hit or miss. For the water bottle with "Home & Living" selected, it still gave me #Tumblr and #Vintage—tags more suited to a decorative item than a practical water bottle.
Pricing: Free (3 searches/day), Pro at $5/month. Best for: Beginners who want the simplest possible tool and only list a few items per day. Try ShopeeTags.io

FAQ

Do Shopee hashtags actually help with search ranking?

Yes, but not as much as your product title. Shopee's search algorithm weights the title most heavily, then the description, then hashtags. Think of hashtags as a support layer—they help your product appear in related searches and can improve discoverability when buyers filter by tag.

How many hashtags should I use per Shopee listing?

Shopee allows up to 10 hashtags per listing. Use all 10. Data from Shopee's Seller Education Hub shows that listings using 8-10 relevant hashtags consistently outperform those using 3-5.

Can I copy hashtags from top-selling competitors?

You can look at competitor tags for inspiration, but copying them directly is a bad strategy. Top sellers already dominate those tags. Instead, use a mix of high-volume tags and niche-specific tags where you can actually rank. A tool like PostBoost AI or SellerPic helps you find the niche ones faster.

Are hashtags the same across Shopee, Lazada, and TikTok Shop?

No. Each platform has its own search behavior. Shopee buyers search with straightforward keywords (#PowerBank). TikTok Shop buyers engage with trend-driven tags (#TikTokMadeMeBuyIt). A good hashtag strategy adjusts per platform.

Is a free hashtag generator enough, or should I pay?

If you list fewer than 5 products a day and only sell in English, a free tool works. If you list across multiple platforms, sell in multiple languages, or value your time, the $3.90/month for PostBoost AI (or similar) pays for itself in about one hour of saved work.
